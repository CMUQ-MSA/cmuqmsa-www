Trust is the work

How this brief is made

Every edition is drafted by an MSA editor and checked by a second reviewer. We distinguish verified reporting from estimates, link evidence beside each summary, and date every review.

  1. 01
    Source carefully

    Each crisis needs at least two substantive sources, including a primary humanitarian source. Instagram, X, and Reddit posts add context but never stand alone as evidence.

  2. 02
    Review every link

    Donation pages must be official, secure, operationally relevant, and rechecked for each edition.

  3. 03
    Correct transparently

    Material corrections are dated and recorded. Old editions remain available so readers can see what changed.

See something we should correct—or have a source or donation organization to suggest? Email the MSA editorial team. Suggestions are reviewed before publication.
